How It Works

in_Custody From Custody to Career
1

Track

While people in custody work on vocational projects, staff log the skills they develop as they go — replacing paper-based, manual record-keeping with one system.

2

Visualise

Those skills are displayed back to the participant on an interactive dashboard — showing what they've learned, how far they've come, and what's still ahead.

3

Match

The platform maps those same skills onto real job opportunities in the market — giving each participant a concrete sense of where their training could actually lead.

4

Build confidence

Presenting skills and progress in an interactive, visual way is designed to lift confidence and motivation — both linked to lower reoffending risk on release.

5

Support staff

Every step above also removes work from staff — one system to manage the whole training process, instead of scattered manual tracking.

Research Goal

Part of the project is aimed at researching the tool's influence on confidence levels among people in custody, and how it helps them find work after release — including its wider effect on reoffending rates and employment outcomes across the UK.
